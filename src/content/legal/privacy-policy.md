## Data we collect

- **When you take a screening:** your full name, email address, birth date, country, city, and your screening results.
- **If you choose:** an email address for a 12-month reminder, or to receive your report.
- **When you contact us or apply** (school, clinic, charity, research and contact forms): the details you enter, and any files you upload (research form).
- **Payments** (donations and the paid report): processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of payment, but never your card details.
- **Usage data:** pages visited, and device and browser information, collected through cookies only if you agree (see [Cookies and consent](#cookies-and-consent)).

## How we use your data

We use your data to:

- run the screening and show your results
- create and email your report
- send a reminder, if you asked for one
- reply to enquiries and applications
- produce anonymised statistics, for example the total number of people screened
- understand how the site is used and show ads, only with your consent.

We don't sell your data.

## Who we share it with

We use these service providers to process data for us:

- Supabase (database)
- DigitalOcean (application hosting)
- Cloudflare (website hosting and delivery)
- Brevo (sending emails)
- Stripe (payments)
- YouTube (only if you play the video on our home page)
- Google (advertising and analytics, only with your consent)

## How long we keep data

- **Personal details from a screening:** deleted automatically after a maximum of 7 days.
- **Reminder email:** kept until the reminder is sent or you unsubscribe.
- **Enquiries and applications:** kept as long as we need them to respond and manage the relationship.
- **Anonymised statistics** (no personal details): kept indefinitely.

## Children's data

EyeCheck.app is designed for users aged 3 and up. A parent or guardian should enter a child's details and take the test with them. By doing so, they give consent on the child's behalf.

We collect only what's needed for the screening, and delete it after 7 days.

## Your rights

You can:

- ask to access or correct your data
- delete your data, using "Delete my data" on your results page or by emailing us
- withdraw your consent
- unsubscribe from emails at any time.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can contact Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

## Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), limit who can access data, and keep personal data only for a short time.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.
